About

I am a materials scientist based in Tartu, Estonia, and a Master's student on the EIT-labelled AMIR programme (Advanced Materials Innovative Recycling) at the University of Tartu. My background is in biology, chemistry and environmental science and engineering, and my work focuses on recycling, materials and the circular economy. I have taught biology and chemistry to secondary school students, and I also explain science to the public, including the life cycle of disposable electronics.
