01 / About me

Research with an environmental purpose.

I work across environmental science, sustainability, and materials-related research, with a focus on translating evidence into useful insights for communities, organisations, and decision-makers.

My academic background spans biology, chemistry, environmental science, and engineering. My research interests include municipal solid waste, plastic pollution, environmental quality, resource recovery, and circular-economy approaches.

I am currently pursuing the Erasmus Mundus EIT Advanced Materials and Innovation (AMIR) master’s programme, with studies at the University of Tartu and the University of Bordeaux.
